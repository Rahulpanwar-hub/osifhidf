Key NCERT Observation: Inside a current-carrying solenoid, field lines are parallel and uniform. Inserting a soft iron core dramatically increases field strength ($B = \mu_0 \mu_r n I$).

Clock Face Rule: Looking at an end of the coil, if current flows clockwise, that face acts as a South Pole; if anti-clockwise, it acts as a North Pole.
